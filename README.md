## About me

I am an **AI/Software Engineer with 6+ years of experience**, focused on Generative AI and production LLM systems. I design and implement autonomous agents, retrieval-augmented generation workflows, backend APIs, full-stack applications, data pipelines, and observable cloud-native services.

- Architecting multi-step agents with **LangGraph** and **LangChain**
- Building semantic retrieval and RAG systems with **Qdrant**
- Integrating multiple model providers with reliable fallbacks and guardrails
- Delivering backend, data, observability, and Kubernetes infrastructure
- Based in **Brasilia, Brazil**
- Portuguese (native), English (advanced), Spanish (intermediate)
- MBA in Software Architecture and Bachelor's degree in Computer Science
