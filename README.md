## About

I am a software engineer with 6+ years of experience, focused on Generative AI and LLM systems. I design and implement end-to-end agents, retrieval-augmented generation workflows, backend APIs, full-stack applications, data pipelines, and observable cloud-native services.

My current work centers on multi-step reasoning with LangGraph, semantic retrieval with Qdrant, multi-provider model integrations, and the engineering required to make AI systems scalable, reliable, and traceable in production.

- Based in Brasilia, Brazil
- Native Portuguese, advanced English, intermediate Spanish
